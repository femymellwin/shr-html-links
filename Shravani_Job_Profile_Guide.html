01 / Your route to launch

Start here

Build one accurate professional story, then adapt it to the fields each job site actually uses. Start with Naukri and LinkedIn for India, then Naukrigulf, Bayt and GulfTalent for the Middle East. Add specialist sites selectively rather than maintaining many incomplete profiles.

This is a private working guide, not a public resume.
The supplied resume is the basis for the candidate content. The guide has not created accounts, uploaded documents, verified personal credentials or submitted applications. Shravani should approve the wording and control her own logins, passwords and verification codes.
1

Verify the facts

Confirm employment dates, current status, formal title, notice period, location preferences and certificate evidence. Resolve the highlighted items before publishing.

2

Publish the core profiles

Use the copy buttons for headlines, summaries, experience and skills. Complete structured fields as well as uploading a resume.

3

Make the profiles work

Set relevant job preferences, choose visibility deliberately, create alerts, apply to suitable jobs and send a few specific recruiter/referral messages.

How to read the labels

Resume-derived means the supplied document reports the information, not that it has been independently verified. Confirm first marks missing facts or credentials. Recommendation marks proposed positioning, priorities or search routines. Numbered source links refer to official websites checked on 2 October 2026.

Every copy block has a measured character count, not a claim about a platform's maximum. Use the live counter shown by the site. Desktop paths are described where official guidance supports them; wording, categories, optional fields and mobile layouts can vary. Authenticated account screens have not been inspected.

What is included in the complete ZIP

The self-contained HTML guide, a plain-text copy pack, a printable draft resume template, a credential-verification worksheet, an official-source register and the original resume. The guide works offline; opening job sites or verification links requires internet access. The original resume contains personal contact details: keep this package private.

What to emphasize and what to avoid

Emphasize

Do not add without evidence

AWS expertise, advanced Python, Argo CD, production RAG ownership, enterprise architecture ownership, team-management claims, invented uptime or cost savings, or a claim that all ten listed certificates are active.

India-first, Gulf-second execution

A practical starting allocation is about 70% of search effort to India and 30% to the Middle East, reflecting the stated preference, not a market statistic.
