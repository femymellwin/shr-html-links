01 / What the product does

An integrated local-first finance and delivery-operations application for multiple operating companies, combining general ledger, payables and receivables, procurement, project billing, time approvals, HR/payroll-related workflows and group reporting.

  • Operating units are configured for India (INR), Singapore (SGD), UAE (AED), USA (USD) and Japan (JPY); a separate GROUP/AED entity supports selected consolidation reporting.
  • Business capabilities cover general ledger double-entry postings, AR/AP invoices, customer/vendor payments, bank reconciliation, budgets, period controls, projects and billing methods, timesheets, procurement, expenses, leave and payroll.
  • Finance reports derive from posted transactions; project profitability also labels estimated, not-yet-posted resource cost separately from booked amounts.

03 / End-to-end business and technical flow

  1. Company and contract setup. An authorized user selects an operating company, configures customers/vendors, projects, resource rates and applicable contract or billing method.
  2. Time capture. A resource enters weekly timesheets; the records are scoped to project, company, and user.
  3. Approval and customer evidence. Managers approve submitted time; externally obtained customer-approved hours/reference are recorded when required by billing rules.
  4. Billable calculation. Contract type, approved quantity, project caps and rate/PO restrictions are validated before the application creates a draft AR invoice.
  5. Accounting entry. Finance reviews and posts the AR invoice; the application creates balanced general-ledger postings.
  6. Cash and reporting. A customer payment is recorded against the invoice and bank; operational dashboards and financial reports reflect posted entries.
  7. Group view (optional). Authorized users inspect group consolidation snapshots and the supported paired-intercompany recharge eliminations.

05 / RAG: Is retrieval-augmented generation used?

Not implemented in supplied project

No retrieval-augmented generation pipeline, embedding layer, vector index, LLM call or evidence-to-prompt generation step was found in the submitted application. SQL queries that produce reports are not automatically RAG. A future read-only reporting assistant could retrieve authorized ledger data and use it as context, but that is a proposed extension only.

A report query is not RAG unless retrieved evidence is supplied to a generative model for answer generation.

06 / MCP: Is Model Context Protocol used?

Not implemented in supplied project

LedgerOne has documented scoped, read-only REST integration endpoints and service credentials. Those endpoints are not, by themselves, an MCP server. Adding MCP would require an explicit MCP-compatible host/server exposing approved tools/resources and their permission mapping.

07 / Data entities and access controls

Representative business data

  • Companies, users, service credentials and company assignments
  • GL accounts, balanced journals, postings, currencies and FX rates
  • Customers/vendors, contracts, purchase requests/orders and AP/AR invoices
  • Projects, resources, weekly timesheets, approval records and billing rules
  • Employees, leave requests, payroll periods and settlement records
  • Intercompany recharges, report results and consolidation snapshots

Privacy, security and governance

  • Company ownership and role-based access are checked by API modules; navigation respects permissions.
  • Financial postings must balance, and invoice/payment workflows enforce state and currency rules.
  • Integration APIs use separately scoped read-only service access; users should not share admin credentials.
  • The application documentation explicitly warns that statutory accounting/payroll compliance and full IFRS consolidation are not certified.

09 / How to explain it in an interview

60-second product narrative

LedgerOne brings finance, projects and workforce operations into one company-aware platform. We built the browser experience and FastAPI services around actual accounting and approval rules, linking approved delivery hours to draft invoices, posted GL entries and reporting. Unlike our AI products, LedgerOne currently uses conventional rules and APIs—not RAG or MCP.

How are entities separated?

Users have explicit company permissions and records are assigned to a company; both API validation and the frontend company selector respect the selected scope.

How does project time become AR?

Submitted time is approved, customer-approved quantities are captured where required, contract rules calculate billable amounts, then a draft AR is posted and later paid.

Does it use AI or RAG?

No. The submitted build contains no LLM, RAG or MCP integration. It includes deterministic financial logic and ordinary REST data integrations.

What should we not claim?

Avoid saying it is externally audited, production-deployed, a React app, fully statutory compliant or AI-powered without independent evidence.
